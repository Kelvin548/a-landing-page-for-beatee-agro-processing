Who We Are

Crafted with Passion, Delivered with Pride

At BEA-TEE AGRO PROCESSING, we believe great food should nourish both body and soul. From farm to factory, our dedicated team works with passion and care to produce Gari that meets the needs of local and international markets.

  • 100% natural cassava with no preservatives or additives
  • Hygienically processed and sealed for maximum freshness
  • Available in White Gari, Yellow Gari, and Fortified varieties
  • Trusted by homes, retailers, and wholesalers across Ghana
  • Supporting local farmers and communities

"At BEA TEE AGRO PROCESSING, we believe that every meal should bring happiness. That's why we pour our passion and effort into every pack of Gari we produce."

— Juliana Afi Yamenu, Founder
What We Offer

Our Product Range

Every grain of Gari tells a story of dedication, purity, and Ghanaian craftsmanship.

Classic

White Gari

Pure and natural, made from carefully selected cassava tubers. Smooth texture, clean aroma, and authentic taste.

  • 100% natural, sun-dried and roasted
  • Long shelf life, hygienically packed
  • Fine, medium, and coarse textures
  • Perfect for soakings, Eba, and more
Premium

Yellow Gari

Enriched with pure red palm oil for extra flavor, color, and nutrition. Crunchy, golden, and deeply satisfying.

  • Naturally colored with palm oil
  • Rich in Vitamin A and healthy fats
  • Distinct golden color and great taste
  • Ideal for Gari foto, Gari jollof
Specialist

Fortified & Custom Gari

Enriched with essential vitamins and minerals — ideal for schools, hospitals, and nutrition-focused organizations.

  • Fortified with essential micronutrients
  • Custom packaging & branding available
  • Coconut, Margarine & Soya varieties
  • Wholesale & export supply

Our Standards

Quality is Our Identity

We maintain strict hygiene from farm to factory. Every batch of Gari passes through careful sieving, roasting, and drying to achieve the perfect texture and flavor.

  • Farm-Level SelectionOnly the finest, fully matured cassava tubers are chosen from trusted local farms.
  • Strict Hygiene ProtocolsEquipment is regularly sanitized and workers are trained in responsible food-handling practices.
  • Precision Roasting & DryingCareful sieving, roasting, and drying ensures perfect, uniform texture and rich flavor.
  • Airtight Sealed PackagingFreshness is locked in — your Gari stays crisp, aromatic, and delicious for months.
