Our Story

A Legacy Born from Faith & Family

Beatee Agro-Processing, located in Warakese, Mampong-Ashanti, stands as a remarkable example of perseverance, innovation, and family-driven entrepreneurship. Its roots trace back to 1981, when Stella Anane, a visionary woman and devoted mother, began roasting gari using a small rounded pan — primarily to feed her family.

What started as a modest effort to sustain her household soon blossomed into a small-scale gari production venture. The factory was initially named Nyame Na Ayɛ Gari Factory, reflecting Stella's deep faith and gratitude to God.

When official registration became necessary, her daughter Juliana Afi Yamenu took up the mantle. The name was updated to DAGBA Gari Factory and formally registered on the 11th of November 2014.

As the matriarch Stella Anane passed away, Juliana fully took over operations, supported greatly by her own daughter Beatrice Ayawo Nyamadzorse, who helped modernise and grow the business. The factory was renamed Beatee Agro-Processing — inspired by Beatrice's vital role — a name that is personal, meaningful, and full of family pride.

Today, Beatee Agro-Processing stands as a symbol of resilience, generational effort, and innovation in local agro-processing — demonstrating how traditional skills can be passed down, reimagined, and scaled for the future.

Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver healthy, tasty, and affordable Gari to every household, while empowering local farmers and supporting sustainable agriculture. We add value to cassava through innovation, fortification, and sustainable processing — creating decent work opportunities for the youth, enriching communities, and delivering high-quality foods to markets across Africa and beyond.

🌍

Our Vision

To become a leading African brand in nutritious cassava-based foods, transforming local harvests into global nourishment. We envision a future where every pack of BEA TEE Gari carries the legacy of Ghanaian excellence to tables across the world — nourishing families near and far.
